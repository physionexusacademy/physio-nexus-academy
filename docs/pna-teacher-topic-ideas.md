## 4. Quality rules (so "genius" also means correct)
* Every lesson: English plus Roman Urdu, 3 or more understanding checks, 4 flashcards, 2 sources, and stop signs where relevant (the existing `.pna` format and validators enforce this).
* [!] topics: reviewed by a clinician (and a pharmacist for medicines) before publishing; no doses without a cited source; say plainly where the evidence is weak or disputed (for example blood flow restriction, cycle-based training, isometrics for pain).
* Emergency lessons teach recognition and escalation, not treatment beyond the physio's scope.
* [PK] topics: involve a person from the community or religious-scholar review for prayer and Ramadan content; do not generalise across patients.
* Build in batches of about 10 lessons, one pull request per batch. Since the lesson index moved to a static file, a lesson batch no longer grows the Worker; only learning paths do (a few KiB).
