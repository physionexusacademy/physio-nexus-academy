## D. Teaching formats for the research track
* "Appraise this paper": real-style short papers with deliberate flaws to find.
* "Design the study": pick a clinical question, choose the design, defend it.
* "Which number is lying?": spot misleading statistics in short extracts.
* Mini-projects: a clinic audit, a case report, a survey, each with a template.
* Statistics widgets: effect size and sample size sliders, confidence-interval simulator, forest-plot reader.

## E. Quality rules
Bilingual (English + Roman Urdu), at least 3 checks, 4 flashcards, 2 sources, stop signs; [!] topics reviewed before publishing; no doses without a cited source; say plainly where evidence is weak or disputed; research lessons use real, citable guidance (for example the reporting guidelines named above) and never invented statistics.
