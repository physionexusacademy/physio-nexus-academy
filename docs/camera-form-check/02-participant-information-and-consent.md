# English

## Why are we doing this study?
PNA RehabVerse is a website that gives people exercises for neck and back problems. We are testing a new feature that uses a phone camera to count repetitions and to estimate the angle of your neck or back during an exercise. Before it is ever used with patients we want to know how accurate it is. You are not being tested; the tool is.

## Why are you being asked?
You are a healthy adult (18 to 60 years) without a recent neck or back problem, and you may be able to help us.

## Do you have to take part?
No. It is your choice. If you say no, or if you change your mind later, nothing happens to you and you do not have to give a reason. [If you work or study with the researchers: saying no will not affect your job or your studies in any way.]

## What will happen?
- It takes about one hour. A physiotherapist will be with you all the time.
- You do eight gentle neck and back movements (for example turning your head, bending sideways, a hip hinge, a bridge on a mat and a bird-dog position), four times each.
- Each movement is held for about three seconds while the physiotherapist measures it with a standard instrument and a phone camera measures it as well.
- You then rest and repeat the movements once more, so we can see how repeatable the measurements are.
- For a few movements we ask you to make a small, safe, deliberate mistake (for example lifting a shoulder) to check that the tool notices it.
- You answer a few short questions about how easy the tool was to use and how comfortable you felt.

You wear your normal comfortable clothes. You will never be asked to remove any clothing. You can wear what you normally wear, including loose clothes and a head covering, because we want the tool to be tested in real life.

## What does the camera do?
The camera shows you your own live picture on the screen. The phone works out the position of your joints while you move. **No video, photo or body-position record is saved or sent anywhere.** The only things we write down are numbers (angles, counts, times) next to a code, not your name.

## Are there any risks or discomforts?
The movements are gentle and are the same kind that people do in everyday life and in normal exercise programmes. You may feel a little muscle tiredness or soreness afterwards. If you feel pain, dizziness or tingling at any time, tell the physiotherapist and we stop at once. You can ask for a female or a male assessor and for a chaperone to be present.

## Are there any benefits?
There is no direct benefit to you. Your help lets us find out honestly whether the tool is accurate enough to be offered to patients, and for which movements.

## What happens to the information?
- Your name is not written on any measurement. You get a study code. The list linking codes and names is kept on paper (or in a protected file) by the researcher and nobody else sees it.
- Only the research team and an independent analyst see the numbers.
- The results will be published as group results only. Nobody will be able to identify you.
- Information is kept for [TO COMPLETE: period] and then destroyed.
- You can ask us to remove your numbers at any time before the final analysis.

# Roman Urdu (Urdu ka Roman likhai)

## Hum yeh study kyun kar rahe hain?
PNA RehabVerse ek website hai jo gardan aur kamar ke masail ke liye exercises deti hai. Hum ek naya feature test kar rahe hain jo phone ka camera istemal karke exercise ke dauran repetitions ginta hai aur gardan ya kamar ka angle andaza karta hai. Marizon par istemal se pehle hum jaanna chahte hain ke yeh kitna durust hai. Aap ka imtihan nahi ho raha; tool ka ho raha hai.

## Aap se kyun kaha ja raha hai?
Aap ek sehatmand baalig (18 se 60 saal) hain jin ko haal mein gardan ya kamar ka koi masla nahi, aur aap hamari madad kar sakte hain.

## Kya aap par shamil hona zaroori hai?
Nahi. Yeh aap ki marzi hai. Agar aap mana karein, ya baad mein iraada badal lein, to aap ko koi nuksan nahi hoga aur wajah batane ki zaroorat nahi. [Agar aap hamare saath kaam ya parhai karte hain: inkaar se aap ki naukri ya parhai par koi asar nahi hoga.]

## Kya hoga?
- Taqreeban ek ghanta lagega. Poore waqt ek physiotherapist aap ke saath hoga.
- Aap aath halki gardan aur kamar ki harkatein karenge (jaise gardan ghumana, side par jhukna, hip hinge, mat par bridge aur bird-dog position), har ek chaar baar.
- Har harkat ko taqreeban teen second rokenge. Physiotherapist standard instrument se naapega, aur phone ka camera bhi naapega.
- Phir aap aaram karenge aur yahi harkatein ek baar aur dohrayenge, taake pata chale ke naap kitne dohraye ja sakte hain.
- Kuch harkaton mein hum aap se ek chhoti, mehfooz, jaan-boojh kar ki hui ghalti (jaise kandha uthana) karne ko kahenge, taake dekha ja sake ke tool usay pakadta hai.
- Aakhir mein chand mukhtasar sawal: tool istemal karna kitna aasaan tha aur aap ne kitna aaram mehsoos kiya.

Aap apne aam aaramdeh kapde pehnein. Aap se kabhi kapde utarne ko nahi kaha jayega. Dheele kapde ya sar ka dupatta/hijab, jo aap aam taur par pehnte hain, wohi theek hai, kyunke hum tool ko asal zindagi jaisi halat mein test karna chahte hain.

## Camera kya karta hai?
Camera screen par aap ki apni live tasveer dikhata hai. Phone harkat ke dauran aap ke jodon ki position ka andaza lagata hai. **Koi video, photo ya jism ki position ka record save ya kahin bheja nahi jata.** Hum sirf numbers (angle, ginti, waqt) ek code ke saamne likhte hain, aap ka naam nahi.

## Kya koi khatra ya takleef hai?
Harkatein halki hain aur wohi qism ki hain jo log rozmarra zindagi aur aam exercise programmes mein karte hain. Baad mein halki muscle thakan ya dard ho sakta hai. Agar kisi bhi waqt dard, chakkar ya sunn-pan mehsoos ho to physiotherapist ko batayein, hum foran ruk jayenge. Aap female ya male assessor aur chaperone ki maujoodgi maang sakte hain.

## Kya koi fayda hai?
Aap ko seedha koi fayda nahi. Aap ki madad se hum imaandari se jaan sakte hain ke tool marizon ko dene ke liye kitna durust hai, aur kin harkaton ke liye.

## Maloomat ka kya hoga?
- Aap ka naam kisi naap par nahi likha jata. Aap ko ek study code milta hai. Code aur naam ki list kaghaz par (ya mehfooz file mein) researcher ke paas rehti hai aur koi aur nahi dekhta.
- Sirf research team aur ek azaad analyst numbers dekhte hain.
- Nateeje sirf group ke taur par shaya honge. Aap ko koi pehchan nahi sakega.
- Maloomat [TO COMPLETE: muddat] tak rakhi jayegi, phir tabah kar di jayegi.
