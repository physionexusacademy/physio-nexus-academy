# What this pack is

A complete set of **draft** documents for an ethics-committee submission of a small pilot study (8 to 10 healthy volunteers) of the camera feature, plus a drafted-but-not-submitted second part for a supervised patient pilot. Everything is written so that **you** fill the `[TO COMPLETE: …]` fields (names, registration numbers, dates, site, committee, funding, jurisdiction, retention periods) and sign. Nothing in the pack claims that the tool is accurate: the study exists to find that out.

**Check against your committee's own forms first.** Committees differ. Use their application form and copy the answers from document 04's summary table; keep these documents as the attachments. Where a committee asks for something not here, tell me and I will draft it.

# Decisions that are yours

1. **Whether to store anything beyond numbers** (document 15). The base submission says no video, no images, no landmark coordinates. Google Drive is technically possible, but it changes the ethics profile; the options and the safeguards are in document 15.
2. **Your commercial interest** (documents 01, 04, 12): state it plainly; the committee will ask.
3. **Which committee and which national rules apply** (documents 01, 07, 11): the drafts deliberately do not assume them.
4. **Retention periods** (documents 02, 07, 15).
5. **Who the independent analyst and the blinded assessor are** (documents 01, 08, 12).

# What I have not done and cannot do for you

- I cannot sign, date or stamp anything, choose your committee, or confirm local legal requirements.
- I have not checked the references against the originals (each document says so); please do.
- The Roman Urdu text is a careful draft, not a certified translation; the SUS translation is a draft.
- The tool has **not** been shown to be accurate; the documents say so and the study measures it.
- Numbers such as distances, heights and thresholds in document 03 are provisional until a feasibility trial on real phones.

# After approval, in order

Freeze the software and record the hashes (document 13, section 12) → register (document 13) → feasibility trial on real phones (confirms views and distances) → recruit (document 14) → run Part A with the blinded protocol (documents 01, 10) → independent analysis (document 08) → apply the decision rules → only then prepare Part B (documents 05, 06).
