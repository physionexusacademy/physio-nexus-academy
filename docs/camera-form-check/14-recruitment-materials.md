A physiotherapist will be with you all the time. # 4. Screening call or conversation script

1. "Thank you for your interest. This takes about five minutes. I will tell you about the study first and you can stop me at any time."
2. Summarise the study in plain words (use the lay summary, document 04): one hour, eight gentle movements twice, physiotherapist present, camera and instrument, nothing recorded, voluntary, can stop.
3. "Would you like me to send you the full information sheet to read before you decide? There is no hurry."
4. Ask the eligibility questions of Form 1 (document 10) **only after** the person has agreed to continue; write nothing that identifies them on the form; use a study code.
5. If eligible and still interested, book a time at least [TO COMPLETE: for example 24 hours] after they have received the information sheet; remind them that they can still decline on the day.
6. If not eligible, thank them; do not explain the reason in a way that suggests a health problem ("this study needs people who have had no recent neck or back pain").
7. If the person is a student or staff member of the investigators: "You are free to say no and it will have no effect on your work or studies." Check that there is no reporting or assessment relationship; if there is, thank them and do not enrol them.
