# English

## Why are we doing this study?
Your physiotherapist has given you exercises for your neck or back. Our exercise website has a new feature that uses the camera of your phone to count your repetitions and to show how your neck or back is moving while you exercise. We want to find out whether it is safe, easy to use and helpful in real patients. You are not being tested; the feature is.

## Why are you being asked?
You are an adult patient of this clinic and your physiotherapist thinks the feature may suit some of your exercises.

## Do you have to take part?
No. **Your treatment stays exactly the same whether you say yes or no.** Your exercises, how often you do them and the care you receive do not change. If you say yes you can change your mind at any time, for any reason, without telling us why and without any effect on your care. The camera is optional every time you exercise.

## What will happen?
- Your physiotherapist will show you how to set up your phone and will be with you for your first camera session.
- You do the exercises you were already given. The camera shows you your own live picture, counts your repetitions, may time your holds and may give short tips such as "Keep your shoulders level."
- It cannot see everything. For example it cannot see the curve of your spine or whether your deep muscles are working, and it will say so.
- [TO COMPLETE: choose] You use the camera only in the clinic / You may also use it at home for [TO COMPLETE: period]. At home you can contact [TO COMPLETE: contact] with any question.
- At the end we ask you a few short questions about how easy and comfortable it was. This takes about [TO COMPLETE] minutes.

## What does the camera do?
The camera shows you your own picture on your own screen. The phone works out where your joints are while you move. **No video, photo or body-position record is saved or sent anywhere.** Your physiotherapist sees only numbers (for example how many repetitions you did and for how long you held), never a picture of you.

## Are there any risks or discomforts?
You do the same exercises you were already given, so the risks are the same as for your normal exercises: a little muscle tiredness or soreness. The feature might give a wrong number or a tip that does not suit you. **Stop and tell your physiotherapist if anything hurts, feels dizzy or tingles. This message is on the screen every time.** The feature will never tell you to go further into a movement. You can ask for a female or male physiotherapist and for a chaperone.

## Are there any benefits?
We cannot promise a benefit. Some people find the feedback helpful. Your help lets us decide whether the feature is good enough to offer to other patients.

## What happens to the information?
- Your name is not written with the results. You get a study code. The list linking codes and names is kept by the researcher and nobody else sees it.
- Only the research team and an independent analyst see the numbers.
- Results will be published as group results only. Nobody will be able to identify you.
- Your normal clinic records are kept as usual and are not changed by this study.
- Information is kept for [TO COMPLETE: period] and then destroyed. You can ask us to remove your numbers at any time before the final analysis.

## Who is running this study?
[TO COMPLETE: researcher and organisation.] The researcher [TO COMPLETE: confirm] has a commercial interest in the exercise website. An independent person analyses the results, and all results are reported, whether good or bad.

# Roman Urdu (Urdu ka Roman likhai)

## Hum yeh study kyun kar rahe hain?
Aap ke physiotherapist ne aap ko gardan ya kamar ke liye exercises di hain. Hamari exercise website mein ek naya feature hai jo aap ke phone ka camera istemal karke aap ki repetitions ginta hai aur exercise ke dauran dikhata hai ke aap ki gardan ya kamar kaise hilti hai. Hum jaanna chahte hain ke asal marizon mein yeh mehfooz, aasaan aur mufeed hai ya nahi. Aap ka imtihan nahi ho raha; feature ka ho raha hai.

## Aap se kyun kaha ja raha hai?
Aap is clinic ke baalig mareez hain aur aap ke physiotherapist ke khayal mein yeh feature aap ki kuch exercises ke liye munasib ho sakta hai.

## Kya aap par shamil hona zaroori hai?
Nahi. **Aap ka ilaaj bilkul wohi rehta hai chahe aap haan kahein ya na.** Aap ki exercises, unki tadaad aur aap ko milne wali dekh-bhaal nahi badlegi. Agar aap haan kahein to bhi aap kabhi bhi, kisi bhi wajah se, wajah bataye baghair iraada badal sakte hain aur aap ke ilaaj par koi asar nahi hoga. Har baar exercise karte waqt camera ka istemal aap ki marzi par hai.

## Kya hoga?
- Aap ka physiotherapist aap ko dikhayega ke phone kaise rakhna hai aur aap ke pehle camera session mein aap ke saath rahega.
- Aap wohi exercises karenge jo pehle se di gayi hain. Camera aap ko aap ki apni live tasveer dikhata hai, repetitions ginta hai, holds ka waqt naap sakta hai, aur chhote mashware de sakta hai jaise "Kandhe barabar rakhein."
- Yeh sab kuch nahi dekh sakta. Misal ke taur par woh aap ki reerh ka curve ya yeh nahi dekh sakta ke aap ke gehre muscles kaam kar rahe hain ya nahi, aur woh khud yeh bata dega.
- [TO COMPLETE: chunein] Aap camera sirf clinic mein istemal karenge / Aap ise ghar par bhi [TO COMPLETE: muddat] tak istemal kar sakte hain. Ghar par kisi sawal ke liye [TO COMPLETE: rabta] se rabta karein.
- Aakhir mein hum aap se chand mukhtasar sawal poochte hain ke yeh kitna aasaan aur aaramdeh tha. Is mein taqreeban [TO COMPLETE] minute lagte hain.

## Camera kya karta hai?
Camera aap ki apni tasveer aap ki apni screen par dikhata hai. Phone harkat ke dauran aap ke jodon ki jagah ka andaza lagata hai. **Koi video, photo ya jism ki position ka record save ya kahin bheja nahi jata.** Aap ka physiotherapist sirf numbers dekhta hai (misal ke taur par aap ne kitni repetitions ki aur kitni der roka), aap ki tasveer kabhi nahi.

## Kya koi khatra ya takleef hai?
Aap wohi exercises karte hain jo pehle se di gayi hain, isliye khatre wohi hain jo aap ki aam exercises ke hain: halki muscle thakan ya dard. Feature ghalat number ya aisa mashwara de sakta hai jo aap ke liye munasib na ho. **Agar kuch bhi dard kare, chakkar aaye ya sunn-pan ho to ruk jayein aur apne physiotherapist ko batayein. Yeh paigham har baar screen par hota hai.** Feature aap ko kabhi nahi kahega ke harkat mein aur aage jayein. Aap female ya male physiotherapist aur chaperone maang sakte hain.

## Kya koi fayda hai?
Hum fayde ka wada nahi kar sakte. Kuch log feedback ko mufeed paate hain. Aap ki madad se hum faisla kar sakte hain ke feature doosre marizon ko dene ke qabil hai ya nahi.

## Maloomat ka kya hoga?
- Aap ka naam nateejon ke saath nahi likha jata. Aap ko ek study code milta hai. Code aur naam ki list researcher ke paas rehti hai aur koi aur nahi dekhta.
- Sirf research team aur ek azaad analyst numbers dekhte hain.
- Nateeje sirf group ke taur par shaya honge. Aap ko koi pehchan nahi sakega.
- Aap ka aam clinic record pehle ki tarah rehta hai aur is study se nahi badalta.
- Maloomat [TO COMPLETE: muddat] tak rakhi jayegi, phir tabah kar di jayegi. Aakhri tajziye se pehle aap kabhi bhi apne numbers hatane ko keh sakte hain.
